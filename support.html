Support

Questions, problems, or a story you'd love to read next? Write to support@journeva.app. We answer in English, German or French, usually within two working days.

Frequently asked

Which level should I choose?

Pick the one where you understand most of a story without effort, then read the same story one level up. A1 is complete beginner, A2 elementary, B1 intermediate, B2 upper-intermediate. You can change your level any time in Settings, and switch levels inside any story with the badge at the top.

What is free and what needs Premium?

Free: ten starter stories at every level, every other story at A1, word translations, saved words and practice sessions of up to ten words. Premium: every story at every level, narration on every story, and unlimited practice. Premium costs €2.99 a month or €24.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial, and can be shared with your family through Apple's Family Sharing.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In the app, go to Settings → Manage Subscription; or on your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Journeva. If you cancel during the free trial you are not charged. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Premium until the current period ends.

I bought Premium but the app says Free.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. If you're on a family member's plan, make sure Family Sharing includes subscriptions. Still stuck? Email us with the approximate purchase date; never send your receipt's payment details.

Narration sounds robotic, or a language has no narration.

Journeva uses the voices built into iOS. Better voices are free to download: iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → pick the language → download an "Enhanced" or "Premium" voice. If no voice is installed for a language, narration for that language is unavailable until you add one.

How do new stories arrive?

On their own. The app checks for new stories when you open it and downloads them in the background — you never need an App Store update for new content. Settings → Story updates lets you check right away. If you're offline, everything you already have keeps working.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your device only — see the privacy policy. Settings → Reset progress clears favourites, saved words, history and streak while keeping your level and narration speed. Deleting the app removes everything.

The daily reminder doesn't show up.

Check that notifications are allowed for Journeva in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that Focus modes aren't silencing it. The reminder is scheduled on your device; it never needs an internet connection.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — Journeva is for iPhone and iPad. If you'd use it on Android, send a one-line email to hello@journeva.app with "Android" in the subject; we count them. Every story is free to read at A1 on the web in the meantime.

Can I request a story or report a mistake in one?

Please do. Every story is reviewed before it's published, and corrections are folded into the next weekly update. Email the story title, the language and level, and what to fix.
