Onigiri

A thousand-year-old Japanese lunchbox trick that needs only rice, salt, and your own two hands.

Level A1 · Beginner

In Japan, lunch often fits in your hand. Onigiri are rice balls. Inside, there is a small surprise filling. Outside, there is crisp seaweed.

People have made onigiri for more than a thousand years. You need only three things: warm rice, a little salt, and your two hands.

Wet your hands with a little water. Put a little salt on your palms. Take some warm rice and make a small hole in the middle.

Put a small piece of filling in the hole — maybe salted salmon, or a pickled plum. Close the rice around the filling. Press gently. Shape it into a triangle.

Wrap a strip of seaweed around the bottom. Your lunch is ready — made tonight, for tomorrow, with your own hands.
