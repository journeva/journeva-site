Rest Your Eyes

A rule with three twenties in it, for anyone whose eyes burn by the end of a screen-filled day.

Level A1 · Beginner

Daniel worked on a screen every day. By four o'clock, his eyes hurt. Every day, the same problem.

He went to an eye doctor. The doctor looked at his eyes. "Your eyes are fine," she said. "But you need a new habit."

"Every twenty minutes, stop," she said. "Look at something far away. Twenty meters is good. Look for twenty seconds."

Daniel tried it. Every twenty minutes, he looked out the window. He looked at a tree. He looked at the sky.

By Friday, his eyes did not hurt. The rule was small. Three numbers: twenty, twenty, twenty. But it worked.
