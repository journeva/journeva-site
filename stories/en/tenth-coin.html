The Tenth Coin

Tomas is always broke by the twentieth, until the woman at the kiosk shows him where her first coin goes.

Level A1 · Beginner

Tomas works at a café in Veldra. On the first of the month, his pockets are full. On the twentieth, they are empty. "Where does it go?" he asks.

Mrs. Halden sells newspapers at the kiosk. She has done this for forty years. She never looks worried. "I have one rule," she says.

She takes out a jar. "Of every ten coins, one goes in the jar. The first coin, not the last. Before rent, bread and coffee."

"Why the first?" asks Tomas. "The last coin never survives the month. The first coin is safe. It is gone before you spend it."

Tomas tries it. The first month is hard. A year later, the café closes for a week. Tomas does not worry. He bought a week of not worrying.
