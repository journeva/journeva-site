The Jar of Stones

A teacher fills a jar with big stones, small stones, and sand — and asks what it means.

Level A1 · Beginner

One day, a teacher brought a big empty jar to class. She also brought big stones, small stones, and a bag of sand.

First, she put the big stones in the jar. The jar looked full. "Is the jar full?" she asked. "Yes!" said the students.

Then she poured in the small stones. They rolled between the big stones. "Is it full now?" she asked again. The students were not sure.

Last, she poured in the sand. It filled every small space. Now the jar was really full.

"This jar is your life," the teacher said. "The big stones are important things — family, health, goals. Put the big stones in first. Then everything else finds its place."
