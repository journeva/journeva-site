Four-Count Breathing

Turns out a ship captain's trick for rough weather works just as well ten minutes before an exam.

Level A1 · Beginner

So it's ten minutes before Lena's exam. Her heart is beating fast, and her hands are shaking.

Her grandfather is an old ship's captain. He has a trick for bad weather. Breathe in for four, hold for four. Breathe out for four, hold for four.

Lena tries it in the hallway. In, two, three, four. Hold, two, three, four.

After a minute, her hands are calm. She feels ready. And it's free, so you can use it anywhere.
