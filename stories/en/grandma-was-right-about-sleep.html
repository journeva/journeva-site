Why Grandma Was Right About Sleep

"Go to bed early," her grandma always said, and it turns out science agrees.

Level A1 · Beginner

Every night, Elena's grandma says, "Go to bed early. Your body says thank you." Elena thinks it's just an old habit.

Years later, Elena is a busy student. She goes to bed late. She's tired, and she gets sick a lot.

So she tries Grandma's advice for one week. She sleeps eight hours every night. After a week, she feels like a new person.

Doctors agree: good sleep helps the brain and the heart. Sometimes simple advice is the best.
