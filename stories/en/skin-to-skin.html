Skin to Skin

A hospital runs out of incubators, and a doctor tries the oldest one there is: a mother's chest.

Level A1 · Beginner

In 1978, a hospital in Bogotá, Colombia, has a problem. Many babies are born too early. They are very small. The hospital does not have enough incubators.

A doctor named Edgar Rey tries something new. He lays a baby girl on her mother's chest, skin to skin. The baby stays warm. Her heart is calm.

People call it kangaroo care, like a kangaroo and its baby. A big study in 2021 found it saves lives: deaths of small babies fell by a quarter. In 2022, doctors everywhere began to use it.

Today, Amira has her baby in a different hospital. Her son is big and healthy, not early at all. Still, the midwife lays him on her chest, skin to skin, for one hour. Soon he finds the milk alone.

The best incubator in the world costs nothing. It was there all along.
