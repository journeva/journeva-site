Three Good Things

A small green notebook and one habit at bedtime slowly change how a hard day feels.

Level A1 · Beginner

Amira has a hard day. Her aunt gives her a small green notebook. "Tonight, write down three good things from today," she says. "And why."

Amira almost laughs. Three good things? Not today!

But she thinks about it. The bus is on time. The soup is good. A friend sends a funny message. She writes all three.

The next day, she looks for good moments, and she finds more. Every night, she writes three things. Slowly, her days feel better.
