Never Useless

A line Charles Dickens gave to a character in his last complete novel, and the boy he never stopped writing for.

Level A1 · Beginner

When Charles Dickens was twelve, his father went to prison. Charles left school. He put labels on jars of shoe polish, all day. He never forgot it.

In 1865, Dickens finished a book: "Our Mutual Friend". Bella says she is useless. John answers: "No one is useless in this world who lightens the burden of it for any one else."

The book is full of people the world called useless. Jenny has a bad back. She makes dresses for dolls. A boy reads to an old woman.

Being useful is not about what you make. It is about whose day you make lighter.

Selin listens to her brother for twenty minutes. That is not useless. Whose day did you make lighter today?
