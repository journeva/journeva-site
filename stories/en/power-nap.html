The Twenty-Six-Minute Nap

The most famous nap in history was taken in a cockpit with scientists watching, and it was short on purpose.

Level A1 · Beginner

In 1994, scientists at NASA ask pilots to sleep. On long flights, one pilot takes a short nap in the cockpit. A scientist takes notes.

The naps last about 26 minutes. After the nap, the pilots work 34 percent better. They are 54 percent more alert. The numbers are very clear.

Why so short? Sleep gets deeper every minute. After thirty minutes, you feel slow and heavy, like underwater. A short nap of ten to twenty minutes is different. You wake up light and clear.

Mateo is a baker. He starts work at four in the morning. After lunch, he is always tired. For years, he felt lazy for wanting a nap. Now he knows the rule.

Early afternoon, twenty minutes, an alarm on the table. A nap late in the day can disturb your sleep at night. So keep it early, keep it short, and feel no guilt.
