The Sauce That Takes All Morning

In 1891 every publisher turned down Pellegrino Artusi's cookbook, so he printed it himself, and inside was one of the first written recipes for the sauce of Bologna.

Level A1 · Beginner

It's 1891, and Pellegrino Artusi is seventy-one. He writes a cookbook, but every publisher says no. That hurts. So he prints it himself.

Inside is one of the first recipes for Bologna's famous sauce. His sauce has veal, pancetta and butter. No tomato yet!

Today in Bologna, it goes like this: cook beef until it's brown. Add wine, tomatoes and milk. Wait three hours.

Put the sauce on tagliatelle. Not spaghetti! The publishers are forgotten. The sauce is not.
