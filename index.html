Questions

Which level should I start at?

The one where you understand most of a story without effort. A2–B1 suits most people who've had some school lessons. You can change it anytime, and switch inside any story.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your progress stays on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything already on your device works without a connection; new stories arrive when you're back online.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — Journeva is for iPhone and iPad. If you'd use it on Android, send a one-line email to hello@journeva.app with "Android" in the subject; we count them. Until then, every story is free to read at A1 on the web.
